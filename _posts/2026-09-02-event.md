### ZooMS-identifying skins associated with the mummified individuals from Uunartoq, Greenland with local high school students
Join us this September for an exciting talk by Luise Ørste Brandt, from the Globe Insititue, University of Copenhagen, who will be discussing a collaborative project with high school students that used ZooMS to identify ancient sources of leather.

Contact us for the Zoom link to join live, or watch later on [PAASTA's YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@PAASTAcommunity).
