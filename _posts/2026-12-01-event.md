### PAASTA Annual Symposium🍝🎙️🎄
PAASTA is hosting the annual symposium on the 1st of December at [EuPA conference](https://proteomic-forum.com/) this year. <br/>
The theme will be **_Proteins beyond the Present_**: please prepare for 10-minute presentations. It is a hybrid event, so we welcome presentations from anyone, anywhere. The symposium will be free as usual.

Practical details see below ⬇️ 

### Practical details
- Date: 01-12-2026
- Location: Hybrid
- Application deadline: 27-09-2026 [**Sign-up form**](https://forms.gle/gjkGLBtweM4HVDCJ7)

All talks will be recorded and shared on the [PAASTA YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@PAASTAcommunity/videos) later.
